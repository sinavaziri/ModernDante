The first image is a painting in the style of a Gustave Doré steel engraving glazed with oil color: a cross-section of the frozen lake at the center of the earth from Dante's Inferno, a classic of world literature. A great winged giant, seen from the front, is frozen into a slab of ice up to his chest, both fists at his mouth; below the slab his two shaggy legs hang into a dark cavern.

The second image is Gustave Doré's own engraving of this giant, and it is the reference for how much hair his body has.

In the first image the giant's upper body is far too hairy: it is buried under a long shaggy coat like a bear. Repaint only his chest, shoulders and arms above the ice to match Doré: a powerful, heavily muscled body of bare grey-blue skin, the muscles of the shoulders, upper arms, forearms and chest clearly modelled with fine hatching, and only a light covering of short, coarse hair, a little on the shoulders and upper back, along the forearms, and down the centre of the chest, no more than in Doré's engraving. Where his chest meets the ice, let the hair grow somewhat thicker and longer along the bottom edge, so it leads naturally to the shaggy legs below. Keep his size, his outline and his pose exactly: same width of shoulders, same position, elbows on the ice, both fists at his mouth.

Do not change anything else: his head, hair, horns, face, fists and wings, the slab of solid ice, the two legs under the ice with their shaggy coat and clawed feet, the cavern with its icicles and spikes, the two small robed figures hanging upside down under the ice, the two tiny robed figures on the crag at the upper right, the frozen lake, and the brown rock at both sides. Same positions, same sizes, same colors.

Crisp fine hatching and cross-hatching, with the engraved linework visible under the color. No text, no letters, no border, no frame, no signature. Do not crop and do not zoom.
