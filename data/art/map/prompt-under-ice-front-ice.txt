The first image is a painting in the style of a Gustave Doré steel engraving glazed with oil color: a cross-section of the frozen lake at the center of the earth from Dante's Inferno, a classic of world literature. A great winged giant is frozen into a slab of ice up to his chest; below the slab his two shaggy legs hang into a dark cavern.

The second image is another painting from the same series, and it is the reference for two things: how the ice is painted, and how the two travellers look.

Make two corrections to the first image and change nothing else.

1. The ice. The slab of ice across the middle is painted with long, flowing, wispy strokes, so it looks like matted hair or fur, and tufts of white hair spread over it where the giant's chest goes in and where his thighs come out. That is wrong. Repaint the whole slab, from the rock wall on the left to the rock wall on the right, as ice like the ice in the second image: hard, clear, blue-white ice broken into angular blocks and slabs with flat faces, straight cracks and sharp edges, some blocks jutting forward out of the cut face and catching the light, with dark blue gaps between them. No flowing lines, no strands, nothing that looks like hair. Where the giant's chest enters the ice, and where his thighs come out underneath, the ice simply closes around him with a clean edge, a few small blocks and a little frost; remove the white tufts and fringes of hair. Keep the slab the same thickness and in the same place, keep the flat frozen lake behind the giant, and keep the icicles hanging from the underside.

2. The two travellers. On the crag of ice at the upper right stand two tiny figures who now look like one blurred clump. Repaint them, at the same small size and in the same spot, as the two travellers in the second image: two separate figures standing upright side by side, one in a long red robe with a red cap, the other in a long blue-grey robe with a wreath of laurel on his head, looking toward the giant.

Do not change the giant in any way: his head, horns, face, fists, chest, arms, wings and both legs with their shaggy coat and clawed feet stay exactly as they are, same size and position. Do not change the cavern with its icicles and spikes, the two small robed figures hanging upside down under the ice, or the brown rock at both sides.

Crisp fine hatching and cross-hatching, with the engraved linework visible under the color. No text, no letters, no border, no frame, no signature. Do not crop and do not zoom.
