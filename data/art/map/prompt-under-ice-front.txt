This image is an unfinished painting in the style of a Gustave Doré steel engraving glazed with oil color: a cross-section of the frozen lake at the center of the earth from Dante's Inferno, a classic of world literature. A great shaggy winged giant, seen from the front, is frozen into the lake up to his chest, both fists at his mouth. Directly beneath him, under the ice, his two massive shaggy legs hang down into a dark cavern, ending in clawed feet.

The giant's upper body and his legs are finished and correct. Do not change them at all: same size, same position, same shape, same shaggy grey coat, same feet. Do not change his head, horns, face, fists or wings either.

What is unfinished is everything between and around the two halves, which were pasted together and must be painted into one scene:

1. The ice. Between his chest and his legs paint one continuous slab of solid, opaque, cloudy white-blue ice running the full width of the scene from the rock wall on the left to the rock wall on the right. Its top is the surface of the frozen lake at the level of his chest; its front is cut straight through, like a geological section; its underside, fringed with icicles, is where his legs come out. The slab is only as thick as it is now between chest and legs. Nothing of his body can be seen inside it. Where the fur of his chest meets the ice, and where his thighs come out of the underside, let the ice grip the fur naturally with frost and small icicles.

2. The cavern. The cavern under the ice now stops in hard dark rectangles at the left and right. Extend it outward to the rock walls on both sides in the same manner: dark blue rock, ice spikes rising from below, icicles hanging from the underside of the slab. Remove the straight edges and the flat dark panels completely. Let the cavern fade into deep darkness along the bottom edge.

3. Keep the two small robed figures hanging upside down under the ice at the right of the legs, the two tiny robed figures on the crag at the upper right, the frozen lake behind the giant with its small pale figures frozen in the surface, the icicles at the top, and the brown rock at both sides.

One light, one palette of cold blues and greys across the whole picture. Crisp fine hatching and cross-hatching, with the engraved linework visible under the color. No text, no letters, no border, no frame, no signature. Do not crop and do not zoom.
