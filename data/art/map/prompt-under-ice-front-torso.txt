The first image is a painting in the style of a Gustave Doré steel engraving glazed with oil color: a cross-section of the frozen lake at the center of the earth from Dante's Inferno, a classic of world literature. Above the ice a great winged giant, seen exactly from the front, leans forward with both fists at his mouth. Through the ice his waist is dimly seen, and below the ice his two legs hang straight down into a dark cavern. The second image is a reference for the look of his legs: long, coarse, shaggy grey hair in thick locks, and large clawed feet.

The giant does not read as one body, and that must be corrected. His upper body is smooth, scaly skin and fairly narrow at the waist, but his legs are far too big for it, wider than his whole torso, and covered in a different shaggy texture, so the two halves look like different creatures at different scales.

Repaint the giant's whole body so it is one shaggy creature at one scale, as the poem describes him, with matted hair down his sides. Give his chest, shoulders, upper arms, belly and flanks the same coat as the reference: long, coarse, shaggy grey hair in thick locks, thickest down his sides, with the muscles still showing through; only his face, fists and wings stay as they are. Then repaint the hips and legs in proportion to that torso: the hips exactly as wide as the waist seen through the ice and no wider than his ribcage, each thigh about half the width of the hips, the outer edges of the thighs continuing the lines of his sides straight down without stepping outward. The legs hang straight down, side by side, seen from the front, about as long as the distance from the top of his head to his hips, with the same shaggy coat and with clawed feet in proportion that end well above the bottom edge. From shoulders to feet it must be one continuous coat of hair in one color under one light.

Keep everything else exactly as it is: his pose, head, horns, face, fists and wings, the icicles, the frozen lake and its broken slabs with small pale figures, the band of ice seen in section, the cavern with its icicles and spikes, the two small robed figures hanging upside down under the ice at the right of the legs, the two tiny robed figures on the crag at the upper right, and the dark rock at both sides. Same positions, same sizes, same colors.

Crisp fine hatching and cross-hatching, with the engraved linework visible under the color. No text, no letters, no border, no frame, no signature. Do not crop and do not zoom.
