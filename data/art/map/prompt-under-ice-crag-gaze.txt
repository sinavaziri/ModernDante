This is a detail of a painting in the style of a Gustave Doré steel engraving glazed with oil color, illustrating Dante's Inferno, a classic of world literature: the poets Virgil and Dante stand on a crag of ice.

Out of the picture, to the left and a little BELOW the two men, at about the height of their knees, is the thing they have come to see: a giant frozen in a lake. At present both men lift their chins and look upward into the dark, over the giant's head. Change only their heads and gaze so that both are plainly looking at the giant: toward the left edge of the picture and slightly downward.

Virgil, at the left, in the blue-grey robe with the green laurel wreath on short curly hair, beardless: his head is in profile facing left, chin lowered, eyes cast down and to the left. His near arm is raised a little and points down toward the left, showing Dante the giant.

Dante, just behind him at the right, in the red robe and red cap over a white coif with ear flaps, with his hooked nose and jutting chin, beardless: his head is in profile facing left and bowed slightly forward, looking down and to the left past Virgil's shoulder in dread, one hand still on Virgil's arm.
